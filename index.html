AI Usage Policy

Our guidelines for responsible and effective use of artificial intelligence tools.

1

Policy Statement

At JB Graphics, we embrace AI technology as a tool to enhance creativity, improve efficiency, and deliver exceptional results for our clients. This policy outlines our approach to using AI responsibly and transparently.

Our Commitment: We use AI tools to augment our design process while maintaining human oversight, creative control, and quality standards. All final deliverables are reviewed and approved by our experienced team members.

Acceptable Uses:

  • Research and ideation support
  • Workflow automation and process optimization
  • Content drafting and editing assistance
  • Design exploration and concept generation
  • Code generation and technical problem-solving

Guidelines:

  • Never input confidential client information into public AI systems
  • Always verify AI-generated content for accuracy and appropriateness
  • Maintain transparency with clients about AI tool usage when requested
  • Respect intellectual property rights and avoid copyright infringement
  • Use AI to enhance, not replace, human creativity and judgment

Data Privacy: We do not share client data, proprietary information, or sensitive business details with AI systems without explicit permission. Internal tools and secure platforms are used when working with confidential materials.

This policy is subject to updates as AI technology and best practices evolve. Last updated: July 2026
